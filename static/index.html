    How to Use

    1. Start Recording: Click the "Start Recording" button and grant camera/microphone permissions
    2. Take Your Swings: The app will automatically detect the impact sound ("wack!") of each swing
    3. Stop Recording: Click "Stop Recording" when you're done
    4. Review Clips: Each swing will be automatically clipped and ready to review
    5. Compare: Select 2 or more clips and click "Compare Selected" to watch them side-by-side with synchronized playback

    Tips for Best Results

    • Make sure your device's microphone can clearly hear the swing impact
    • Position the camera so your full swing is visible
    • Take 3-5 swings per session for easy comparison
    • Wait a second or two between swings for cleaner detection
