    How to Use

    1. Record Video: Click "Start Recording" and grant camera/microphone permissions
    2. Take Your Swings: The app automatically detects the impact sound of each swing
    3. Stop Recording: Click "Stop Recording" when done
    4. Review Clips: Watch individual clips (2 seconds each: 1s before/after impact)
    5. Choose Analysis Mode: Select Side-by-Side, Overlay, or Position Compare
    6. Select Clips: Pick the appropriate number of clips for your chosen mode
    7. View or Download: Analyze your results and download if needed

    Tips for Best Results

    • Make sure your device's microphone can clearly hear the swing impact
    • Position the camera so your full swing is visible
    • Take 3-5 swings per session for easy comparison
    • Wait a second between swings for cleaner detection
